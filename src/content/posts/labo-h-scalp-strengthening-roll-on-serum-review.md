I picked up the LABO·H Scalp Strengthening Roll-On Serum at Olive Young,
and got lucky: it came bundled in a set with a sample-size shampoo,
which I'll review separately. This post is just about the roll-on serum
itself, since that's the one I've actually had time to put through its
paces.

## What's actually in it

The formula leans on
<a href="/vocab#yusangyun" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">유산균</a>
(*yusangyun*), probiotics, in the form of a fermented lysate, alongside
ceramide, PDRN, biotin, and collagen. The pitch is
<a href="/vocab#maikeurobaiom" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">마이크로바이옴</a>
(*maikeurobaiom*), microbiome, balance for the
<a href="/vocab#dupi" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">두피</a>
(*dupi*), scalp — treating it like skin with its own bacterial
ecosystem to keep in check, rather than just something hair grows out
of.

## The click-and-roll application

Each pen has a clicker on the bottom: twist it and it releases a
cooling, almost menthol-like serum through a roll-on tip. I roll it
directly onto my scalp after washing my hair, section by section, then
massage it in with my fingertips. Koreans would call that tingle
<a href="/vocab#siwonham" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">시원함</a>
(*siwonham*), a cool, refreshing sensation, and it's honestly the best
part of the routine. A few clicks per section is plenty, and the
sensation afterward is genuinely nice, not just tolerable.

## Early results with hair shedding

I deal with a fair amount of
<a href="/vocab#talmo" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">탈모</a>
(*talmo*), hair loss, so I went in with low expectations. Roll-on
scalp serums promise a lot and rarely deliver fast. But after just a
few days of consistent use, I've noticed the shedding has actually
lessened. It's early, and I want to see if that holds up over weeks
rather than days, but it's the first product in a while that's given me
a reason to keep watching.

## Verdict

A well-designed applicator with an ingredient list that actually
targets the scalp instead of just the strands, and early results that
have kept me using it daily. The 15 ml size means it won't last forever
at daily use, but for now, this has earned a permanent spot in my
post-wash routine.

## What the show is

The full title is **Pokémon The Orchestra: 꿈과 모험, 그리고 만남의 기적**
(*Dreams, Adventure, and the Miracle of Encounters*). Per Pokémon
Korea's announcement, it reworks music from the main *Pokémon* game
series into orchestral
<a href="/vocab#medeulli" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">메들리</a>
(*medeulli*), medleys, running from the upbeat adventure themes to the
more emotional tracks. Game footage plays on screen during the show,
and the organizers note some scenes are staged differently from the
actual games. Inven Global also reports a special segment previewing
the series' upcoming adventures.

Pokémon Korea pitches it as a show for every generation of fan, from
people who started on Game Boy to kids playing now. No setlist has been
published, but going by the poster, expect a trip through all nine
generations.

This is the third time Pokémon Korea has done this. It works with
**Flasic**, a company that specializes in video game concerts, and its
Flasic Game Symphony Orchestra:

- **2023:** *From the Land of Myths* (신화의 땅에서), National Theater of
  Korea's Haeoreum Theater
- **2024:** *A Treasure-like Journey* (보물 같은 여정), Sejong Center
  Grand Theater, built around *Scarlet and Violet*. It was conducted by
  Flasic's head, Jin Sol.

Pokémon Korea hasn't named the conductor or orchestra for 2026 yet.
The concert is the music piece of **Pokémon Mega Festa 2026**, the
year-long 30th-anniversary celebration that already gave Seoul the
[Jamsil pop-up](/blog/pokemon-mega-festa-jamsil-popup) and
[Hanbok Pikachu](/blog/hanbok-pikachu-pokemon-seoul-pop-up).

## Dates, venue and seat prices

- **Dates:** Saturday, November 28 and Sunday, November 29, 2026
- **Times:** 2:00 p.m. and 7:00 p.m. both days (four shows)
- **Venue:** <a href="https://map.naver.com/p/search/KBS홀" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-brand-700 underline">KBS Hall</a>, Yeouido, Seoul (여의도 KBS홀)

## How booking works

<a href="/vocab#yemae" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">예매</a>
(*yemae*), booking, opens **Thursday, October 15 at 8:00 p.m. KST** on
[Ticketlink](https://www.ticketlink.co.kr), and the rules are strict:

- **Two tickets max per account.**
- **Your ticket is also your store pass.** You need it to get into the
  merch store at the venue, and you can only make one purchase per
  ticket.
- **Don't lose it.** Damaged or lost tickets won't get you into the
  concert or the store.

Just four shows in a single hall for a 30th anniversary means
this will probably be a
<a href="/vocab#piketing" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">피켓팅</a>
(*piketing*), "blood ticketing." Make your Ticketlink account well
before 8 p.m., log in early and have your payment method ready.
Foreign cards and identity checks can be hit or miss on Korean
ticketing platforms, so test the checkout before sale time if you're
booking from abroad.

Pokémon Korea hasn't posted the running time or age rating yet. For
reference, the 2024 show ran 130 minutes including a 20-minute
intermission and was rated ages 8 and up. Check the Ticketlink listing
once it goes live.

## Getting to KBS Hall

The closest subway stop is **National Assembly Station (국회의사당역)**
on Line 9. Take **Exit 4**, and the hall is a short walk west. Pokémon
Korea specifically asks people to use public transportation because
parking is tight. If you're new to the subway, our
[Seoul transportation guide](/blog/getting-around-seoul-transportation-guide)
covers T-money cards and how the lines work.

Yeouido is an easy place to fill the rest of the day. Yeouido Park is
right next to the hall, and The Hyundai Seoul mall is a short ride away
if you want somewhere warm to wait for the evening show. It'll be
late November, so bring a real coat.

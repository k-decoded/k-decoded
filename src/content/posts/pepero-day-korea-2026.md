Line up four chocolate biscuit sticks and you've got 11/11. That's the
idea behind **빼빼로데이** (*ppaeppaero dei*), the November day when people
in Korea give each other boxes of Pepero.

This year, the boxes have some competition from what's packed alongside
them. CU has Tamagotchi merchandise, GS25 has a Shin-chan film camera,
and 7-Eleven has a character pop-up at a shopping mall. All of this
started with a snack you can pass across a desk. [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/convenience-stores-turn-pepero-day-into-a-character-merch-showdown/12947951)

## Who gets a box?

Friends and partners both do. If you're meeting a friend for coffee on
**Wednesday, November 11**, a regular box is enough to join in. Pick a
flavour they'll like or open it and share it over coffee.

You might also see **수제 빼빼로** (*suje ppaeppaero*), handmade versions
decorated with chocolate, sprinkles or icing. It's the same recognisable
shape, with room to make a few sticks in someone's favourite colours.

## What you'll see in stores in 2026

Start with the convenience store you already pass on your way out. Look
for **한정판** (*hanjeongpan*, limited edition) and **굿즈** (*gutjeu*,
merchandise) if you're after a particular collaboration. Check the side
of the box for what's included, especially if the picture on the front
shows several designs.

- **CU** is leaning into Pokémon and Tamagotchi. If you're already
  visiting Seongsu or Hongdae, the chain has featured Pokémon at CU
  Seongsu Dessert Park and Tamagotchi at its AK & Hongdae branch.
- **GS25** has gone for objects you can use after the biscuits are gone,
  including an Anpanman tape-measure keyring and a Crayon Shin-chan film
  camera.
- **Emart24** has packaging based on *The Remarried Empress*, the Korean
  webtoon adapted for Disney+. [Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/convenience-stores-turn-pepero-day-into-a-character-merch-showdown/12947951)

## One pop-up to put on your Jamsil list

The **7-Eleven × ZO&FRIENDS Pepero Day pop-up** is on **B1 of Lotte
World Mall** through **November 11**. ZO&FRIENDS is the character brand
created by G-Dragon and IPX. The lineup includes a plush keyring in a
roasted chestnut hat, plus pouches and bags.

The pop-up has a gift for qualifying purchases of **₩30,000 or more**,
with supplies handed out first come, first served. Check the current
details if you're going for a specific item. [The Asia Business Daily](https://www.asiae.co.kr/en/article/2026110209392995215)

## What to say when you hand it over

**빼빼로데이라서 샀어요. 같이 먹어요!**<br />
*Ppaeppaero deiraseo sasseoyo. Gachi meogeoyo!*<br />
“I bought these for Pepero Day. Let's share!”

If you're buying a particular set, **이거 아직 있어요?** (*Igeo ajik
isseoyo?*) means “Do you still have this?” Show the cashier a photo of
the exact box when you ask. There can be a lot of different boxes on
the same shelf.

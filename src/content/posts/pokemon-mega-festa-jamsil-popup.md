If you're in Seoul this week, Jamsil just became ground zero for Pokémon's 30th birthday. Today, August 16, Pokémon Korea opens <a href="/vocab#muleungdowon" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">무릉도원</a>
(*Muleungdowon*) at Lotte World Mall — the newest stop on the citywide
<strong>Pokémon Mega Festa 2026</strong> tour that's been running across
Seoul since May, and the first leg of it to land on this side of the
river.

## Where and when

- **Address:** Lotte World Mall, 1st floor atrium — 300 Olympic-ro,
  Songpa-gu, Seoul (Jamsil)
- **Dates:** August 16 (Sun) – August 31 (Mon), 2026
- **Hours:** 10:30 AM – 10:00 PM daily, though department-store event
  hours can shift, so it's worth double-checking Pokémon Korea's
  Instagram the morning you go
- **Entry:** Free to browse the indoor pop-up store. The outdoor market
  at Arena Square is a different story — it's
  <a href="/vocab#yeyak" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">예약</a>
  (*yeyak*)-only, booked in advance through the LotteON or Lotte
  Department Store Mall apps rather than walk-up (details below)

## What the name means

Muleungdowon is the Korean name for a legendary peach-blossom paradise
lifted from an old Chinese fable — basically Korea's shorthand for "the
kind of idyllic place that isn't supposed to exist." The pop-up leans
into that literally: cloud-and-blossom staging built around the store,
positioning the whole thing as a little pocket utopia dropped into a
Jamsil mall for two weeks.

## The merchandise drop

The headline act is a <a href="/vocab#ing-eo-king" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">잉어킹</a>
(*ing-eo-king*), Magikarp, capsule line built around the tagline "it's
okay to be weak" — a self-aware nod to the Pokémon everyone knows as
the game's designated punching bag before it evolves. The
<a href="/vocab#gutjeu" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">굿즈</a>
(*gutjeu*) lineup covers a lot of ground:

- Glitter-logo T-shirts and embroidered caps
- Phone straps, keychains, and charms
- Capsule toys, plushies, and squeezable stress balls
- Beach towels, pouches, and ice trays
- A Pokémon Center Ditto Transformation plush — the most anticipated
  item of the drop

There's also an <a href="/vocab#ichiban-kuji" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">이치방쿠지</a>
(*ichiban kuji*) draw for the 30th anniversary, "Vol. 1" — the grand
"Last One" prize is a 50 cm Pikachu plush. Both merch batches (1st and
2nd drop) go on sale the moment doors open today, and L.POINT members
get an exclusive bonus Magikarp promo card thrown in with purchase.

## The outdoor Starlight Paradise market

There's a second, separate half to this event: the "Pokémon Starlight
Paradise" Summer Market, taking over Arena Square and the container
zone outside Lotte World Mall on the same August 16–31 dates. It's a
different vibe from the indoor Utopia pop-up — an evening night-market
built around a shimmering, starlit theme, anchored by an oversized
Monster Ball installation, projection mapping, and a massive LED
screen running a Gyarados animation. Around that centerpiece are
booths from brands like Uniqlo and FC Seoul alongside toy and figure
shops, a Capsule Toy Zone on the container area's 2nd floor, and a
lineup of street food and dessert stalls.

A couple of built-in perks: spend over ₩100,000 in the market for a
chance at a limited-edition Magikarp bandana or cup holder, and
following Pokémon Korea's Instagram gets you a free Pokémon sun visor
on a first-come, first-served basis each day. Turn in a "Starlight
Magikarp Wish Card" on your way out for a coupon toward the same
promotional Magikarp card mentioned above (still L.POINT-members-only).

Unlike the indoor store, this half is reservation-only:

- **Book via:** LotteON or Lotte Department Store Mall app
- **Reservation windows:** 1st round opened August 7, 10:30 AM; 2nd
  round opens **August 18, 10:30 AM**
- **Entry slots:** bookable in 30-minute increments from 10:30 AM to
  9:30 PM, with 9:30 PM as the last entry
- **Party size:** the reservation holder plus one guest
- **Limit:** one reservation per account per day
- **Questions:** 070-5038-5377, during regular department-store hours

## Worth knowing before you go

Reserve your market slot early — the second reservation round opens
August 18, and slots are booked per account per day, so it's not
something you can sort out at the door. The indoor atrium store is
walk-up, but since both merch waves drop the instant it opens, getting
there early still matters if a specific item is the goal rather than
whatever's left by afternoon. The whole pop-up — both halves — wraps
on August 31.

## Part of a bigger 30th-anniversary tour

Jamsil is only the latest chapter. Pokémon Korea kicked off Mega Festa
2026 back in May, centered on Seongdong-gu before expanding across the
city: a Pokémon Secret Forest and Game Center at Seoul Forest, a Ditto
Playground and Ditto-themed café in Seongsu-dong, a 30th Anniversary
Party pop-up, plus a ticketed fun run and a Sports Day at COEX. Jamsil
is the first leg to swap Seongdong-gu's forest-and-café setting for a
straightforward mall pop-up — worth factoring in if you're deciding
which stop to prioritize.

fwee built its whole reputation on lip tints, and the Rose Obsession
Stay-Fit line is the one most people mean when they say "fwee tint."
Tested here in two shades: GW04 Call Me Baby and GW03 Coco Rose.

## What "stay-fit" actually means

The name isn't just branding — it's describing
<a href="/vocab#miljakryeok" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">밀착력</a>
(*miljakryeok*), or cling power: how tightly a product grips skin (or in
this case, lips) instead of sliding around or sitting on top. That's the
whole mechanism behind a "transfer-proof" <a href="/vocab#rip-tinteu" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">립틴트</a>
(*rip-tinteu*): you apply it, give it a minute to set, and the pigment
bonds to your lips rather than sitting in a wet layer on top of them.
Once that happens, cups, food, and masks stop picking up color the way
they would with a straight-up lip gloss or bullet lipstick.

## Texture and finish

<img src="/images/posts/fwee-lip-tint-applicator.webp" alt="The doe-foot applicator wand for the fwee Rose Obsession Stay-Fit Lip Tint" class="not-prose my-6 w-full rounded-xl object-cover" />

Both shades go on genuinely lightweight — there's none of the tackiness
you'd expect from something described as a tint. The gloss is minimal by
design, which is the tradeoff for that transfer resistance: a
high-shine, glassy finish is wetter by nature, and wetter means more
likely to transfer. What's left once it sets is a soft
<a href="/vocab#chokchokham" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">촉촉함</a>
(*chokchokham*), or dewiness, rather than an actual wet-look shine — and
it stays hydrating rather than drying, so it doesn't cling to or
emphasize dry, flaky patches the way a lot of long-wear tints do.

## Color payoff

<a href="/vocab#balsaek" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">발색</a>
(*balsaek*) is true to the bottle on both shades straight out of the
tube, and builds if you go over it a second time. Neither shade needed
more than one light layer to show up clearly.

## The shades

- **GW04 Call Me Baby** — a soft, milky nude-peach. Reads more "your lips
  but better" than a defined color statement.
- **GW03 Coco Rose** — a deeper, warm-toned rose. More noticeable on the
  lips without tipping into a bold, made-up look.

Both sit inside fwee's 18-shade range for this line, which spans warm,
neutral, and cool rose tones — from very sheer, barely-there shades
through to deeper, more saturated ones, so there's a reasonable spread
whether you want a your-lips-but-better tint or something with more
presence.

## Lasting power

<a href="/vocab#jisokryeok" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">지속력</a>
(*jisokryeok*) held up through a full meal and multiple drinks in
testing, fading evenly rather than patchily — the sign of a tint that's
actually staining rather than just sitting on the surface.

## Verdict

A genuinely transfer-resistant tint that doesn't ask you to give up
comfort to get there — worth it if minimal gloss doesn't bother you, and
worth skipping if a glossy finish is the whole point for you.

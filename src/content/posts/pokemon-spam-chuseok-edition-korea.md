If you've seen a stack of gold-lidded cans wearing Snorlax and Charmander
faces floating around your feed, that's not fan art — it's an actual
grocery product. CJ CheilJedang, the company behind Korea's SPAM, just
launched an official Pokémon collab for <a href="/vocab#chuseok" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">추석</a>
(*chuseok*), and it's turned one of Korea's most traditional gifts into
something people are now scalping on eBay.

## Why Spam, and why this is a big deal

If you're not used to Korean gift culture, a can of meat as a holiday
present sounds like a punchline. It isn't. <a href="/vocab#seupaem" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">스팸</a>
(*seupaem*) has been a genuinely prestigious <a href="/vocab#chuseok" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">추석</a>
and Seollal gift in Korea for decades — boxed <a href="/vocab#seonmul-seteu" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">선물세트</a>
(*seonmul seteu*) of Spam tins are a standard thing to hand a coworker,
landlord, or elderly relative. CJ CheilJedang leaned into that this year
by pairing it with Pokémon's 30th anniversary, out of 260 different
Chuseok gift-set varieties the company released for 2026 — its largest
lineup yet.

## What's actually in the box

The collab is three "SPAM Towers," each themed around one Pokémon:
**Charmander, Ditto, and Snorlax.** Every tower stacks five 200g cans of
Spam Classic wrapped in character packaging, plus a sheet of Pokémon
stickers — 15 cans total if you get all three.

## Pricing and where to find it

- **One tower:** ₩19,900 (~$15)
- **All three towers:** ₩59,700 (~$45)
- **Launch:** September 9, 2026 via KakaoTalk Gift
- **Wider retail:** September 11 at <a href="https://www.cjthemarket.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="text-brand-700 underline">CJ The Market</a>, with E-Mart stock expected to follow

CJ CheilJedang's Chuseok announcement also confirmed the sets would sell
through E-Mart, The Market, and Kakao — so if one channel is sold out,
it's worth checking the others before assuming it's gone for good.

## It's already being scalped

Between the character range and genuinely capped stock, this sold out
fast enough that resellers moved in almost immediately — full
three-tower sets that retail for ~$45 have been spotted on eBay for
around $88, nearly double. If you're shopping from outside Korea,
that's the <a href="/vocab#risel" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">리셀</a>
(*risel*) markup you're paying for convenience — buyers in Korea who can
grab it at CJ The Market or an E-Mart in person are paying half that.

## Good to know before you buy

- **It's a Chuseok item, not a permanent SKU.** Like most Korean holiday
  gift sets, this is tied to the 2026 Chuseok season — don't expect it
  to still be on shelves once the holiday passes.
- **The keychain is random, not a choice.** If you specifically want
  Snorlax or Ditto and not the other, be ready to buy more than one
  complete set.
- **International buyers are stuck with resale or import stores.**
  Sites like Oppa Store and Gmarket Global are shipping the set
  overseas, but you'll pay international shipping plus whatever markup
  the seller adds on top of the ₩59,700 retail price.

The Beauty of Joseon Pore Firming Red Bean PDRN™ Serum is a lightweight
daily serum I picked up at Olive Young. It has become a staple in my
routine because it is easy to use, gentle on my sensitive skin, and
leaves a smooth finish without weighing everything down.

## What “pore firming” actually means

The name focuses on <a href="/vocab#mogong" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">모공</a>
(*mogong*), pores, and <a href="/vocab#tallyeok" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">탄력</a>
(*tallyeok*), elasticity or firmness. The formula combines
<a href="/vocab#bulgeunpat" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">붉은팥</a>
(*bulgeunpat*), red bean, PDRN™ with peptide ingredients, aiming to make
pores look smoother and more refined over time.

I have not seen noticeable pore-firming results yet, so this is one I
am continuing to use rather than calling an instant-results product.

## Texture and everyday wear

The texture is very watery and spreads effortlessly across the skin. It
absorbs quickly, leaving a smooth, comfortable finish with no residue,
stickiness, or greasiness. It layers easily with the rest of my routine,
so I can use it morning or night without having to rethink my other
products.

That fast sink-in feel is what Korean skincare often calls
<a href="/vocab#heupsu" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">흡수</a>
(*heupsu*), or absorption. Here, it means the serum feels like part of
the skin rather than a layer sitting on top.

## Gentle on sensitive skin

This serum is scent-free, which I really appreciate. More importantly,
it has been gentle on my sensitive skin: no irritation, heaviness, or
uncomfortable film after application. Its uncomplicated feel is exactly
why it has stayed in my routine.

The packaging also identifies it as a
<a href="/vocab#gineungseong-hwajangpum" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">기능성 화장품</a>
(*gineungseong hwajangpum*), or functional cosmetic. In Korea, that
label is used for products carrying regulated functional claims.

## The pore-firming results

Pore results tend to take time, and I have not noticed a visible firming
effect yet. Still, the texture and skin feel make it worth continuing
with. It is the kind of serum I enjoy reaching for daily because it is
lightweight, comfortable, and does not interfere with the rest of my
skincare.

## Verdict

A watery, fragrance-free serum with a smooth, non-greasy finish that
works especially well for sensitive skin. The pore-firming claim is
still a work in progress for me, but the easy texture and gentle formula
have already earned it a permanent place in my routine.

Seoul starts putting up Christmas lights while you're still deciding whether you need a winter coat. By mid-November, the department stores in Myeongdong are already lit, and The Hyundai Seoul has built a little piece of New York inside its fifth-floor garden. Come from December 11 and you can add a giant tree at Gwanghwamun and a lantern walk along the stream to the same evening.

## Myeongdong: Two Christmas displays in one walk

Start at **Shinsegae Department Store Main Branch** (신세계백화점 본점). The screen on the outside of the building is called Shinsegae Square, and its Christmas film runs **every night from 5 p.m. to midnight through January 31, 2027**. This year's film follows a bear carrying letters full of Christmas wishes to an ice castle. Take Line 4 to **Hoehyeon Station, Exit 7**. Watch from the pavement, then walk north toward Lotte. Both outdoor displays are free to see. [Shinsegae's Christmas schedule](https://www.shinsegaegroupnewsroom.com/shinsegae-christmas-reveals-landmark-square/) · [Visit Seoul directions](https://english.visitseoul.net/shopping/Shinsegae%20Department%20Store%20%28Main%20Store%29/ENP017598)

At **Lotte Department Store Main Branch** (롯데백화점 본점), about 20,000 bulbs light the façade and a stretch of pavement linking the main store and Avenuel. Look for the **15-metre Heart Light Tree** and follow the lights toward **Euljiro 1-ga Station** on Line 2. Lotte switched on its display on October 30. If you're picking one November evening, do these two back to back and have dinner in Myeongdong afterwards. [Yonhap's opening report](https://www.yna.co.kr/view/AKR20261029187400030)

## Gwanghwamun and Cheonggyecheon: Start at the tree, finish by the water

**Gwanghwamun Market** (광화문 마켓) runs **December 11–January 10** around Gwanghwamun Square and Sejongno Park. There's a giant tree, stalls for seasonal gifts and snacks, and photo spots around the market. Admission is free. It opens at **5:30 p.m.**, closes at **10 p.m. Sunday–Thursday** and stays open until **10:30 p.m. Friday–Saturday**. Take Line 5 to **Gwanghwamun Station, Exit 9**. [Visit Seoul's market guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/events/2026GwanghwamunMarket/ENP0rc1ic)

From the market, walk south to **Cheonggye Plaza** for the **Seoul Lantern Festival** (서울빛초롱축제). The free display follows Cheonggyecheon Stream from the plaza to **Samilgyo Bridge**, with paper lanterns, illuminated sculptures and media art. It runs **December 11–January 31, every evening from 6 to 11 p.m.** Give yourself time to walk down by the water. You'll see more there than you will from the first bridge. [Visit Seoul's lantern festival guide](https://english.visitseoul.net/eventsx/2026SeoulLanternFestival/ENPi0rhml) · [Ministry of Culture festival listing](https://www.mcst.go.kr/site/s_culture/festival/festivalView.jsp?pRo=1&pSeq=13396)

If you have one night between December 11 and January 10, start at Gwanghwamun at 5:30, walk the stream once the lanterns come on, then head south to Myeongdong for Shinsegae. The department store film runs latest, so there's no need to rush the market. New to the subway? See our [guide to getting around Seoul](/blog/getting-around-seoul-transportation-guide).

## Yeouido: Christmas lights you can see in the afternoon

**The Hyundai Seoul's H Village** (더현대 서울 크리스마스 빌리지) has gone full vintage New York this year. The fifth-floor Sounds Forest has red-brick façades, neon signs and a yellow taxi. It's the one to save for a cold afternoon when you've already had enough of standing outside with your gloves off. [Hyundai's look inside H Village](https://www.ehyundai.com/newPortal/group/GN/GNN000020_V.do?seq=573)

The village runs **November 10–December 31, 10:30 a.m.–7:30 p.m.**, with closures **November 26 and December 18**. Entry is free. Check **The Hyundai Hi** for advance reservations before making the trip. From **Yeouido Station** on Lines 5 and 9, follow the underground passage near **Exit 3** to the store's B2 entrance. [Official reservation guide](https://hi.thehyundai.com/contents/780956) · [Store directions](https://thehyundaiseoul.ehyundai.com/about/location)

## Jamsil: Make the Christmas market the evening

**Lotte Town Christmas Market** (롯데타운 크리스마스 마켓) opens **November 19** and runs through **January 3**, **11:30 a.m.–10 p.m.** It's on the **World Park lawn outside Lotte World Tower**, near **Jamsil Station** on Lines 2 and 8. This year's market takes its cues from 19th-century Britain, with a clock tower, a big tree and a carousel. Go after dark if the lights are what you came for. General admission is free, and there's a **₩15,000 Fast Pass**. Check availability for your date before heading across town. [Market details](https://www.thewklyn.com/schedule/?bmode=view&idx=175503419) · [Lotte's booking announcements](https://www.instagram.com/lotteshopping/)

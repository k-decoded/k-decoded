You go in for sushi. You leave wanting a soy-sauce dish.

That's the pitch behind QooQoo's latest Pokémon collaboration, and the dish does have a good trick: pour in the soy sauce and Pikachu's face becomes clearer. Suddenly, a very ordinary part of dinner has become something you're considering taking home.

The Korean sushi-and-roll buffet chain's promotion runs **October 28–December 31, 2026**. Here's what to look for, and what costs extra.

## The keyring is a giveaway. The dish is a purchase.

Paying customers can receive one of five connecting acrylic Pokémon keyrings, assigned at random, while their branch's supplies last. The official poster specifies **one per person**.

The **Pikachu soy-sauce dish costs ₩8,900**, with merchandise purchases restricted to paying restaurant customers. QooQoo lists **Incheon Nonhyeon, Osan and Tongyeong** as branches that don't sell the dish.

This is where two Korean shopping words become useful. **증정** (*jeungjeong*) means something is given as a gift; **구매** (*gumae*) means purchase. Both can appear in the same promotion. Spotting a character on the poster doesn't tell you which one applies.

QooQoo calls these **굿즈** (*gutjeu*), character merchandise. If you've been following our [Pokémon coverage](/blog/hanbok-pikachu-pokemon-seoul-pop-up), this is another way to fit a little Pokémon shopping into a Korea trip — this time over lunch.

## There are tiny Pokémon for your chopsticks, too

QooQoo is also selling three acrylic cutlery-rest designs featuring **싸리용** (*Ssariyong*), the Pokémon known in English as Tatsugiri.

They cost **₩7,900 each** for restaurant customers, with the design supplied at random. You can't choose your favourite. These are sold at selected branches, and QooQoo specifically recommends calling ahead to check availability.

A little Pokémon holding your chopsticks off the table is an easy souvenir to justify. It has a job. Whether that makes buying more than one sensible is between you and your suitcase.

## Already taking photos? There's an event for that

A separate social-media giveaway offers a chance to win a **₩50,000 QooQoo dining voucher**.

To enter, follow the brand's official social account, photograph Pokémon food, merchandise or promotional displays in the restaurant, and upload a feed post with **#쿠우쿠우 #포켓몬** and the official account tagged.

Stories don't count, and private accounts are excluded. The first entry period ends **November 17**, with ten winners announced November 18. Read the [full official entry rules](https://www.qooqoo.co.kr/bbs/board.php?bo_table=promotion&wr_id=148) before posting.

## Before you build lunch around it

Choose the branch first, then check the merchandise. If the Pikachu dish is the whole reason you're going, ask about that item specifically before sitting down. The promotion's end date isn't a promise that stock will last until then.

A useful sentence:

**피카츄 간장종지 아직 있어요?**<br />
*Pikachyu ganjangjongji ajik isseoyo?*<br />
“Do you still have the Pikachu soy-sauce dish?”

For the meal itself, check your chosen branch's current buffet price and opening hours. Decide whether you'd enjoy lunch there even if your first-choice collectible wasn't available. That's a better way to plan an afternoon than arriving hungry with one very specific Pikachu in mind.

Our [Korean restaurant phrases guide](/blog/korean-restaurant-phrases) can help with the rest of the meal. And if you do bring the dish home, at least it's a souvenir you can use at dinner.

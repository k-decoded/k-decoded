Centellian24 built its whole identity on one ingredient, and Madeca Cream
Tight Lifting is the line's flagship: a cica cream doing double duty as a
soothing barrier product and a tightening daily moisturizer. This is one
I've personally used for months and repurchased every time — which, for
a skincare cream, is a more honest signal than any single-use first
impression.

## What "기능성 화장품" actually certifies

The label marks this as a <a href="/vocab#gineungseong-hwajangpum" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">기능성 화장품</a>
(*gineungseong hwajangpum*), or functional cosmetic — a regulated
category in Korea, not just marketing language. A product can only
carry this label if it's been certified for specific functions, and
this one is certified for both whitening and wrinkle improvement. If
you see 기능성 화장품 on a Korean label, it's shorthand for "this claim
had to clear a regulatory bar to be printed here."

## The ingredient behind the whole line

The key ingredient is TECA, an extract of <a href="/vocab#byeongpul" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">병풀</a>
(*byeongpul*) — centella asiatica, sometimes called tiger grass. It's a
land herb, not a marine ingredient, and it's the same soothing,
barrier-repairing plant behind most "cica" skincare, popular for
sensitive or redness-prone skin specifically because it calms rather
than actively exfoliates or resurfaces.

## Texture and everyday wear

In months of actual use, this is genuinely lightweight — a watery
finish rather than a heavy balm, with just enough subtle richness to
work as a daily moisturizer without feeling greasy. That combination is
part of why it's stuck around in the routine: it's light enough for
daily wear but still delivers the deep moisture and radiance the
packaging promises.

## The "tight lifting" claim

<a href="/vocab#tallyeok" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">탄력</a>
(*tallyeok*), elasticity, and <a href="/vocab#ripting" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">리프팅</a>
(*ripting*), lifting, are the two claims doing the heavy lifting in the
name itself. In practice, the tightening effect is real but gradual —
this isn't an in-shower snap-tight sensation, it's a cumulative firmness
that shows up over consistent use, which tracks with what a "total
care" complex cream is actually designed to do.

## Soothing and skin testing

The packaging notes a completed skin <a href="/vocab#jageuk" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">자극</a>
(*jageuk*) test — irritation test — which lines up with the brand's
whole reputation for being gentle enough for sensitive skin. If you ever
see "무자극" on a Korean label elsewhere, that's this same root word:
literally "without irritation."

## Verdict

A lightweight, genuinely soothing daily moisturizer with a tightening
effect that's real but builds slowly rather than dramatically — the
kind of product that earns repurchases through consistency rather than
a flashy first use.

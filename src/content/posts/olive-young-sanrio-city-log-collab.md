If your feed has turned into wall-to-wall Hello Kitty in a hanbok this
week, you're not imagining it. Olive Young and Sanrio just launched
their biggest <a href="/vocab#kollabo" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">콜라보</a>
(*kollabo*) to date — 321 products across 81 brands — and it's already
the kind of drop people are filming unboxing hauls of before it even
finishes selling out.

## What "City Log" actually is

The campaign is called **OLIVE YOUNG City Log**, running August 30
through September 30, 2026 in Korean stores (Olive Young Global's own
promotional pricing window online runs August 30–September 5, KST).
Compared to last year's Sanrio collaboration, that's a 170% jump in
participating brands and a 50% jump in product count — officially the
largest Sanrio collab Olive Young has done.

Every item is a <a href="/vocab#hanjeongpan" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">한정판</a>
(*hanjeongpan*), limited edition — Olive Young has said stock is
limited, and past collabs like this haven't gotten restocks once
they're gone.

## The goods

Plenty of sets bundle in <a href="/vocab#gudeu" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">굿즈</a>
(*gudeu*), branded merch — hair clips, pouches, keyrings, luggage
tags, stickers, and puff cases — alongside full-size and mini product
<a href="/vocab#seteu" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">세트</a>
(*seteu*), sets. For a lot of buyers, the merch is as much the draw as
the product itself — these are the pieces showing up most in TikTok
haul videos.

## The concept: Sanrio characters touring Korea

Instead of just slapping character art on existing packaging, City
Log builds a whole visual concept: Hello Kitty and friends styled
around 12 different Korean regions, each tied to something local —
Jeonju bibimbap, Gongju chestnuts, Udo (Jeju) peanuts, and cities
including Seoul, Busan, and Suwon among them. If you've read our
<a href="/blog/busan-vs-seoul-which-trip-fits-you" class="text-brand-700 underline">Busan vs. Seoul breakdown</a>,
it's a genuinely fun overlap — the same two cities, now with a Sanrio
character assigned to each one's food identity.

Beyond the regional artwork, the collection spans a few other visual
styles entirely: a traditional hanbok Hello Kitty, school-uniform and
leopard-print streetwear versions, and a pixel-art take — enough
range that it doesn't read as one repeated design stamped across 321
products.

## Who's in it

Hello Kitty leads, but the lineup pulls in Kuromi, My Melody,
Cinnamoroll, Pompompurin, Pochacco, and Hangyodon, each paired with
different brands rather than one character per product. Some of the
brand pairings worth knowing:

- **ABIB** — serums and sheet masks, Kuromi and Pochacco editions
- **ANUA** — PDRN serums, creams, and mists, Cinnamoroll edition
- **Goodal** — Green Tangerine vitamin C line, Pompompurin edition
- **rom&nd** — lip tints and cushions, Kuromi and Hangyodon editions
- **Torriden** — Dive-In hyaluronic acid serum sets
- **S.NATURE** — Aqua Squalane moisturizing cream, Hello Kitty edition
- **Mise-en-scène** — Perfect Serum, Pochacco edition
- **HANYUL** — mugwort creams and serums, Hello Kitty edition
- **LABO-H** — Scalp Cooling & No-Sebum shampoo sets, Hangyodon edition

## Why it's blowing up right now

Between the character range, the regional concept, and genuinely
limited stock, City Log has landed as a collector event as much as a
beauty launch — TikTok is full of unboxings and mini hauls, and once
specific keyrings or sets go <a href="/vocab#pumjeol" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">품절</a>
(*pumjeol*), sold out, they tend to stay that way rather than
restocking.

## Good to know before you shop

- **It ships internationally, but not everywhere.** Olive Young
  Global covers a long list of countries, but as of this launch it's
  not shipping to the US.
- **Don't wait on a specific piece.** With genuinely limited stock and
  no restock precedent from past Sanrio collabs, the popular
  character/brand pairings (Hello Kitty and Kuromi especially) are
  the first to disappear.
- **Physical Olive Young stores are themed too** — regional store
  exteriors and photo zones are part of the campaign, so it's worth
  a look in person if you're already near one in Korea, not just
  something you shop for online.

Seongsu already has a pop-up on every corner, but this one has a Pokémon
Center you can walk up to. Musinsa turned the ground floor of its Seongsu
megastore into the town from **Pokémon Pokopia**, the Nintendo Switch 2
life-sim, for Korea's first Pokopia concept store. It's also Musinsa's first
concept store of any kind. If you're in Seoul, you have until **Tuesday,
September 29**.

## What Pokopia is

In Pokopia you play as a
<a href="/vocab#metamong" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">메타몽</a>
(*metamong*), a Ditto that has turned itself into a human. You arrive in a town
where the grass and trees have withered away, and you rebuild it into
somewhere Pokémon want to live. The concept store is built like that town:
fake grass, picket fences, pine trees, a red-roofed Pokémon Center, and
cut-outs of Ditto-as-Lapras, Squirtle, and Bulbasaur standing around the displays.

## Where and when

- **Address:** Musinsa Megastore Seongsu, 1st floor — 62 Seongsu-iro,
  Seongdong-gu, Seoul (a short walk from Seongsu Station, Line 2)
- **Dates:** September 12 (Sat) – September 29 (Tue), 2026
- **Hours:** 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM
- **Entry:** Booking-only for the first nine days has ended. Since September
  21 it's been
  <a href="/vocab#hyeonjang-daegi" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">현장 대기</a>
  (*hyeonjang daegi*), a walk-up queue, so you just show up. Musinsa warns that
  entry can be paused or limited if it gets crowded.

It's been busy. Musinsa said the store's opening weekend brought in over 15%
more visitors than usual, mostly people in their 20s and 30s. The last
weekday and final day are likely to be the worst for queues, so go early.

## What's for sale

Most of the Pokopia goods collection is also on Musinsa's online drop:
T-shirts, hoodies, a varsity jacket, pajamas, socks, keyrings, acrylic
magnets, and a tumbler. The reason to go in person is the in-store-only
stock:

- **Pale Pikachu flat plush keyring.**
  <a href="/vocab#changbaekhan-pikachu" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">창백한 피카츄</a>
  (*changbaekhan pikachu*), Pale Pikachu, is the washed-out cream Pikachu
  that's become the game's breakout character, and this keyring is the item
  most people are there for.
- **Pokémon Center tissue case,** shaped like the red-roofed Center.
- **Mug.**

A second wave went on sale on September 21: a Pokopia playset, Moncolle
figures (human-form Ditto, Pale Pikachu, Ditto transformed into Dragonite,
and Professor Tangrowth), larger figures of Ditto-as-Lapras, Pale Pikachu and
human Ditto, a Moncolle MS Ditto, a Pikachu-and-Pale-Pikachu sticker set, two
cast stickers, and the tumbler.

In-store stock isn't restocked once it's gone, so by the final few days
expect some of it to be sold out.

## The capsule-toy wall

One whole wall is capsule machines, stocked with items you can only get from
them here: 7 grip toks, 4 acrylic dioramas, 8 acrylic shaker keyrings, 6
acrylic keyrings, 10 metal pin badges, and 9 acrylic magnets. They're all
random draws. The machines in Musinsa's photos show prices from ₩8,000 to
₩12,000 a pull, which adds up quickly if you're chasing one design.

## Free gifts with purchase

Every in-store purchase of the goods collection gets two random sticker
designs. Spend more and you unlock one
<a href="/vocab#saeunpum" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">사은품</a>
(*saeunpum*), gift with purchase, per tier:

- **₩30,000+:** a
  <a href="/vocab#tteok-memoji" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">떡메모지</a>
  (*tteok memoji*), a chunky memo block (one per person)
- **₩60,000+:** a glow-in-the-dark Pale Pikachu keyring (one per person)
- **₩100,000+:** one of three reusable shopping bags, at random

Gifts run until they're gone, and after two and a half weeks the top tiers
may already be out. Ask at the counter before you plan your spend around one.

## Worth knowing

- **Go on a weekday morning** if you can. Monday the 28th at opening is your
  best bet for a short queue and full shelves.
- **Stack it with the Hanbok Pikachu events.** HiKR Ground's
  [Antique Shop pop-up](/blog/pokemon-antique-shop-hikr-ground) runs to October
  11, and the [AREX airport train](/blog/pokemon-arex-airport-train-hanbok-pikachu)
  is themed for the same stretch. HiKR Ground is a straight ride from Seongsu to
  Euljiro 1-ga on Line 2, about 20 minutes.
- **Missed it?** Musinsa's online Pokopia drop also runs until September 29,
  though the in-store-only items above aren't part of it.

If your Olive Young shopping list already includes Illiyoon, there's a new name to look for beside the familiar Ceramide Ato products: **MultiBarrier Facial**.

The range launched in Korea on November 16. Amorepacific describes it as Illiyoon's first high-function facial line, extending the brand's body-care expertise into a dedicated facial collection. The headline product pairs **5% dexpanthenol with plant-derived PDRN**. There's also a booster essence and a soothing cream. [Amorepacific's launch announcement](https://www.apgroup.com/int/ko/news/2026-11-16-3.html)

## What actually launched

The three products have different roles in the range.

| Product | Korean name | How the brand positions it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MultiBarrier Facial Repair Cream | 멀티베리어 페이셜 리페어 크림 | The main barrier-care cream, containing 5% dexpanthenol and plant-derived PDRN |
| MultiBarrier Facial Booster Essence | 멀티베리어 페이셜 부스터 에센스 | A first-step product combining toner and essence functions |
| MultiBarrier Facial Soothing Cream | 멀티베리어 페이셜 수딩 크림 | A lighter gel-cream focused on moisture and soothing care |

Amorepacific describes the Repair Cream as soft and comfortable on the skin, while the Soothing Cream has a fresher gel texture. Those are the brand's descriptions. Texture under sunscreen or makeup still needs a proper wear test. [Official product descriptions](https://www.apgroup.com/int/ko/news/2026-11-16-3.html)

## The PDRN is plant-derived

PDRN gets plenty of space on K-beauty packaging, and Illiyoon is making its source part of the pitch. Amorepacific says this formula uses a patented, ultra-low-molecular-weight **plant-derived PDRN** ingredient.

That gives you another word to recognise: **식물성** (*singmulseong*), meaning plant-based or plant-derived. Here, it describes the PDRN ingredient. The launch announcement doesn't establish a vegan certification for the whole product. [Amorepacific's ingredient details](https://www.apgroup.com/int/ko/news/2026-11-16-3.html)

It's an interesting direction for a brand associated with everyday barrier care. Our read is that Illiyoon is trying to make an ingredient-led face cream feel like an easy addition to a familiar shopping basket. Whether it earns a repeat purchase will come down to the finished formula and how it feels in use.

Search for **일리윤 멀티베리어 페이셜 리페어 크림** to find the exact product. The Korean spelling uses **베리어**, which is worth copying if your search brings up the brand's other creams. ## Before it goes in your basket

The first decision is which texture you want to try. The Repair Cream and Soothing Cream are separate moisturisers, so check the full product name before comparing prices.

For the Repair Cream, the questions we'd want a hands-on review to answer are straightforward. How quickly does it settle? Does it sit comfortably under sunscreen? How does it feel by the end of the day? And if you already use an Illiyoon cream you like, does this one give you a reason to switch?

For now, it's a new launch worth knowing about before your next Olive Young visit. Our [guide to Korean skincare labels](/blog/korean-skincare-label-words) explains more of the words you'll see while shopping.

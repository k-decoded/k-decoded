Nongshim is marking 40 years of <a href="/vocab#shin-ramyun" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">신라면</a>
(*Shin Ramyun*) with a two-floor <a href="/vocab#paeopeop-seutoeo" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">팝업스토어</a>
(*paeopeop seutoeo*) in Seongsu — one of Seoul's go-to neighborhoods for
exactly this kind of brand pop-up. We stopped by ourselves, so this is
both a firsthand account of what the first floor is actually like and a
practical guide for planning your own visit.

## What "신" actually means

Before the practical details: the "Shin" in Shin Ramyun isn't a name —
it's the hanja 辛, meaning "spicy." So "신라면" literally translates to
something like "spicy ramyun," which is a much more direct, functional
name than it sounds in English. Once you know that, "신" starts showing
up everywhere on Korean spicy-food packaging as a flavor cue, not just
on this one product.

The pop-up itself is filed under <a href="/vocab#bunsik" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">분식</a>
(*bunsik*) — the general category for casual Korean street/snack food
like tteokbokki, kimbap, and instant noodles. Calling it a 분식 spot
rather than a restaurant sets the expectation correctly: quick,
casual, snack-format food, not a sit-down meal.

## Where and when

- **Address:** 서울 성동구 성수일로4길 52 (52 Seongsu-il-ro 4-gil,
  Seongdong-gu, Seoul) — inside the Stage X Seongsu building, Floors 1–2
- **Dates:** June 16 – November 30, 2026
- **Hours:** Daily, 11:00 AM – 8:00 PM
- **Admission:** <a href="/vocab#muryo" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">무료</a>
  (*muryo*), free — either by advance <a href="/vocab#yeyak" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">예약</a>
  (*yeyak*), reservation, or same-day standby if you just show up

## What's on each floor

The space runs about 120 pyeong (roughly 4,300 sq ft) across two
floors, functioning as what Nongshim calls an "antenna shop" — a
brand-experience space more than a straightforward retail store.

- **1st floor:** merchandise, <a href="/vocab#hanjeongpan" class="font-korean rounded bg-brand-50 px-1 font-semibold text-brand-700 no-underline hover:bg-brand-100">한정판</a>
  (*hanjeongpan*), limited-edition products exclusive to the pop-up, and
  a made-to-order ramyun counter
- **2nd floor:** interactive experiences, including building your own
  noodle bowl and tasting international Shin Ramyun variants that
  aren't sold in Korea's domestic market

## What the first floor is actually like

We only made it to the first floor ourselves — the second floor's
build-your-own-ramyun experience wasn't something we were able to get
into on our visit, so budget for that possibility and don't assume
walk-in access to both floors is guaranteed.

The merch on the first floor had a decent range of different Shin
Ramyun-branded items, but honestly, it came across as a bit
underwhelming once we were actually browsing it — more of a standard
branded-merch table than a standout retail experience. My husband did
manage to pick up a T-shirt from the store, so there's at least
something worth grabbing if merch is your main draw for visiting.

## Worth the trip?

If you're already in Seongsu, it's a genuinely interesting stop — Shin
Ramyun's cultural footprint in Korea makes even a modest brand
activation worth a look. Just go in with tempered expectations on the
first floor specifically, and try to plan ahead if the second floor's
interactive side is what you're actually after.

## Don't confuse this with the earlier pop-up

If you come across older posts mentioning "신라면 카페테리아" (a separate
Nongshim × Zepeto collab that ran at S-Factory Seongsu), that one has
already ended — it was a January–February event, unrelated to this one
beyond sharing the same brand. Shin Ramyun Bunsik The Factory is the
current, active pop-up, running through the end of November.
